# 🚀 Render 1-Click Free Deployment Guide — AuraFlow-AI

Deploy AuraFlow-AI to **Render.com** for **100% FREE** with no credit card required!

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Sign up / Log in to Render
1. Go to **[dashboard.render.com](https://dashboard.render.com/)**.
2. Sign in with your **GitHub Account**.

---

### Step 2: Create New Blueprint Project
1. In your Render Dashboard, click **New +** (top right corner).
2. Select **Blueprint**.
3. Connect your GitHub repository: `Anamika-67/AuraFlow-AI`.
4. Render will automatically detect **`render.yaml`**!

---

### Step 3: Deploy!
1. Give your blueprint a name (e.g. `auraflow-ai`).
2. Click **Apply**.

Render will build both your **Backend FastAPI API** and **Frontend Web Application** automatically for **FREE**!

---

## 🌐 Accessing Your Deployed App

Once the deployment finishes (takes ~2-3 minutes):
- **Frontend App**: `https://auraflow-frontend.onrender.com`
- **Backend API**: `https://auraflow-backend.onrender.com/docs`
